# Limitations — what we cannot do, published

A sales asset, not a liability. Every line here is load-bearing for trust.

- **No revenue.** Pre-revenue, solo founder, no design partners yet. The
  plan to reach five (each ≥2 releases/month) is TODO §Phase 2, not a
  pipeline we pretend to have.
- **No live integrations.** Buzz, jcode, Slack, Linear, CRMs: the protocols
  are mirrored and the swap points proven, but no production traffic has
  touched any of them through Vital.
- **No autonomous external action.** `ACT_IRREVERSIBLE` is human-command,
  always, in year 1. Any change needs legal review + a named officer + a
  written ADR. There is no autonomy path in the matrix, not a disabled one.
- **No compliance posture.** SOC 2, Article 14 evidence packs, DPAs,
  residency: roadmap (Phase 8), not audited, not claimed in any deck.
- **Approximate time travel.** `believedAt` snapshots by transaction time;
  status flips are not versioned. Exact replay is Context Bundles, and only
  Context Bundles.
- **Transfer is the weak point.** Current research (AFTER) says procedures
  specialize to their origin role. Our compiler assumes most cards fail
  transfer — quarantine and decay are the product, not the apology.
- **Young dependencies.** Buzz is ~7 weeks old; QM moves fast (we absorb,
  so it cannot break us, but ideas may rot); jcode has no Windows e2e
  coverage (neither do we — transports stay injectable).
- **Unverified citations stay unverified.** Anything in `idea.md` marked
  unverified (agent-washing study, vertical-capital concentration as a
  headline) is banned from the deck until sourced.
